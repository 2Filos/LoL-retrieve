K'SANTE EASY
RUNES: Phase Rush, Inspiration: Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight. SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE/GHOST. ABILITY
ORDER 3 POINTS IN Q, THEN E MAX. Rush Tiamat.
K'sante is a champion that wants to go for quick pokes with his Q and Grasp while nullifying your response with his shield. For this reason,
it is important that we either catch his shield without the cooldown, or chase him down until it expires and then go for a Q E trade on him.
K'sante has no sustain, so any damage you can build up on him is great. The hard part is wiggling in and out of his Q range and waiting for
him to mess up his shield cooldown. A good K'sante will space perfectly and this will be almost impossible to accomplish so at a certain
point the matchup becomes a farm fest. It is important that we do not fight him on his side of the lane after lvl 6 since he can R us into tower.
remember to use your W on his W stun, he will often chain combo it into his Q.
Garen outscales K'sante in the teamfight really hard so it's safe to just farm and relax in lane if you don't want to look for a kill.
