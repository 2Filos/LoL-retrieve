KLED MEDIUM
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Conditioning, Overgrowth) Sorcery: (Nimbus Cloak, Celerity) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE.
Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Tier 1 Boots into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe.
Kled is a very strong early game champion that will look for a dive on you lvl 3-4 if you make mistakes and take too much damage. This is
why it's important to respect him early game and only go for short trades with Grasp Q here and there. Do not dismount Kled until 6, this is
very important. You only want to dismount Kled when you have kill pressure with Ignite and R, otherwise you are just giving him free HP
back when he remounts if you don't kill him. After about 4 points in Q and Sheen, it will be really easy to burst him when he is off of
Skaarl. It's all about poking him down until he is dismounted and can be one shotted with Q E Ignite R. Garen outscales Kled in side lane
as well, it's just about surviving the early game and getting to a point where you can one shot him while he is dismounted.
