SINGED EASY
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Conditioning/Second Wind, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE/FLASH GHOST
/GHOST TELEPORT. Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip.
Singed is a scaling champion with a weak early game. To fix this, Singed players typically opt to proxy with his superior wave clear early
game. It is important that we thin minion waves when he tries to push them in so that he cannot walk through the tower for free. Singed
is a champion with low sustain, so poking him down is a very efficient strategy. Singed’s runes and build can vary greatly, so keep an eye out
for what he is building. If he has Conqueror Ignite, he has incredibly strong all in potential lvl 1-2 if you are not careful. However, if he has
anything else, he probably won’t do much damage and you can go for auto Qs whenever you like. It’s important that we use W + Q on
Singed’s fling since that is most of his burst and we won’t really get a better opportunity to Q him other than that. It’s important that we
play for the short pokes with Q and do not run into his poison for more than a second unless he flinged us into it and we used W. If
Singed proxies early game before we have Greaves, we don’t really want to chase him and we should mostly just farm at tower. After we
get Berserker’s Greaves, chasing Singed can be a legitimate strategy if we can get his R on cooldown. When Singed uses R, he gets a ton
of stats that we don’t want to fight against so simply getting the ability on cooldown is helpful for your jungler to help punish his proxying. If
his R is active, it’s most likely best to not fight him. If he is staying in lane and fighting you, that is exactly what you want since you will slowly
poke him down with multiple short Q trades. If Singed does not have Phase Rush after Stridebreaker, we can Stridebreaker him as he flings
us and Q him to get an extended trade and hit a full Q E which will be a considerable amount of damage especially since he doesn’t really
have any healing. We can still catch up to him like this even with just Berserker’s Greaves, but it can depend on a lot of things like runes,
items he bought, and how many points we have in Q. Consider all this if you want to chase him and continue spinning after he flings you
while you Q. Make sure you activate Phase Rush after he gets Rylais so that you can actually deal damage to him and remove the slow
for a bit. Splitpushing vs Singed can be a good strategy, but it’s important that you walk with your minions to get them to the tower so that
he can’t proxy them in side lane. He might also try to fling the cannon out of turret range so it’s important to go Hullbreaker so it’s harder
for him to kill it.
