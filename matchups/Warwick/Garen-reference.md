WARWICK MEDIUM
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Second Wind, Overgrowth), Sorcery: Nimbus Cloak, Celerity. SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE
ABILITY ORDER: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe.
Warwick is a champion with sustain comparable to Tryndamere. However Warwick can only mostly heal to half hp so it becomes a lot more
possible to burst him. The main problem is that he can go Barrier which is a huge counter to Garen's burst. For this reason, it is important
to not full commit to all ins vs him and to instead poke him with Q Grasp with Sheen and see what he does when you kite in and out after
getting him really low.to see if you can bait an important cooldown. To actually kill Warwick through Barrier, you need to look at his healthbar
and use abilities to overkill him past what you would normally expect. Otherwise you can just farm Grasp on him and chill in lane since you
outscale Warwick in teamfight hard. Remember to always W his empowered auto (Q) it's the biggest part of his burst. Playing safe all lane
is fine to do since he is really hard to kill.
