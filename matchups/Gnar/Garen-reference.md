GNAR MEDIUM
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE. Ability Order: E MAX. Rush Berserker Greaves
into Ironspike Whip.
Gnar is a strong laning phase champion that is very hard to force a kill on. He has to make a big mistake with his hop cooldown for him to
die, otherwise, it is a Farmville angle for both players in this matchup. Gnar can punish you in the same way you punish him, standing too
far up on the opponents side of the lane without important cooldowns. Gnar can run down Garen with a bunch of autos since he gets a slow
and TONS of movespeed to continue autoing over and over. It is really important that we don’t get ourselves into this situation since it is
realistically the only way we die vs Gnar. His short pokes aren’t very good other than his boomerang, and that ability is dodgeable. We
should also always use W on his passive stacks. Once Gnar is in mega we basically never want to fight him as it makes him super tanky
and strong. When Gnar comes out of mega, he gets this special effect called ‘Tired’ where he is unable to gain rage for a short amount of
time after coming out of mega. This is the optimal time to all in him if he is standing on your side of the lane with missing HP and if you have
Ignite available. Garen outscales Gnar at Stridebreaker, but we still have to respect his extended trades and all in potential with Trinity Force
since it makes him decently tanky with Plated Steelcaps. For Garen to be able to one shot Gnar, he has to already be around 70% HP in
mini form post lvl 6.
