QUINN MEDIUM
RUNES: Phase Rush, Inspiration: Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight. SUMMS: FLASH GHOST ABILITY ORDER: E MAX. Rush
Ironspike Whip into Hearthbound Axe.
Quinn is an unkillable champion in laning phase for Garen because of her vault. It cancels Garen's Q even if he started it mid animation,
slows him, and then pushes Quinn away from Garen. For this reason, we need to fully invest in farming until we have Stridebreaker, which
is the only counter to this interaction. As Quinn kicks Garen and vaults away, you can Stridebreaker as she's on top of you and she will get
hit by the slow. You can get her to vault on you simply by running at her with Q or even just straight up walking at her. If she's good she
will only use it if you Q at her though. At lvl 11 and if the wave is on your side and she vaults like this with you hitting the Stridebreaker slow,
you will one shot her from full HP with Ghost. This is the powerspike you unfortunately have to play for and you must spam ping your team
if she roams with her ultimate. Remember to W when her bird targets you since it adds bonus damage to her autos.
DR. MUNDO EASY
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Conditioning/Second Wind, Overgrowth), Inspiration: Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight.
SUMMS: FLASH GHOST ABILITY ORDER: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe. Buy an Executioner's
Calling after Trinity Force.
Mundo is an incredibly weak early game champion with only really 1 ability to poke with and use, his cleaver. If you stand behind minions,
have good movement, or just straight up W it if he hits it, you can nullify this one ability he has quite easily. If Mundo ever walks up to the
wave to CS or if he misses his cleaver, you can try to punish him immediately with Grasp Q and E. Garen outscales Mundo at 1 item as well
since Trinity Force is so strong vs him, so you don't have to worry too much about winning lane hard. It's still good to try though since Mundo
is pretty easy to punish. Perma splitting is good if you got a decent lead from laning phase.
