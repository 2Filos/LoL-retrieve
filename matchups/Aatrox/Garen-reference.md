AATROX EASY
RUNES: Phase Rush Resolve: Conditioning/Second Wind Overgrowth. SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE/GHOST. ABILITY
ORDER: Q MAX. Rush Boots of Swiftness or Berserker's Greaves. Make sure to buy Executioner's Calling after Stridebreaker for
Mortal Reminder. (Sit on a Zeal and then finish Phantom Dancer after Mortal Reminder)
Push the first two waves with E start for a 2 wave crash so you can safely farm for level 4!
Aatrox is a champion with an incredibly strong early game after level 4 and has the abilities to stun and kite Garen when Garen tries to
charge forward. This is why it's important to use Q max movespeed and rush tier 1 and 2 boots as soon as you can so that you can chase
after him with a quick Q trade and then escape with the remaining movespeed. Building Q max + boots makes it really hard for Aatrox to
hit his Q. A good trading pattern is to use Q E and then cancel spin as soon as possible so that you can auto attack and escape with
Phase Rush. Make sure you use your move speed properly and be mindful of where he will position his Q (you can almost always dodge it
with the amount of movespeed you will have. Keep the trades short, and farm well so you can outscale him at lvl 11 Stridebreaker. Don't
forget your executioner's calling after Stridebreaker! If he catches you with a Q combo, it's best to W as soon as he hits his first Q.
