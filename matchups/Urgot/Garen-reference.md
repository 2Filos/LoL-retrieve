URGOT MEDIUM
RUNES: Conqueror, (Triumph Tenacity Last Stand), Resolve: Conditioning, Overgrowth/Unflinching. SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE.
ABILITY ORDER: E MAX. Rush Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip. Can go Warden's Mail after Berserker's Greaves if you
want to make sure you can't lose early.
Urgot is a champion with an incredibly strong lvl 1 and 2 vs Garen, for this reason you want to start W lvl 1 since he will try to zone you off
with his flip and PTA damage. Once Garen has all 3 of his abilities, it's really easy to win trades into Urgot. You can walk at him and use your
E to start spinning on him. If you see his shield appear on his health bar, he is about to go into his flip dash ability. To counter it, W it so that
the damage and stun is reduced while you continue to spin on him. After your E is fully done running, you can auto Q him as you disengage
from the trade. This is the main trading pattern vs Urgot that works quite well. It may lose you the trade sometimes, but Urgot has no sustain
and all you have to do is sit back and wait as your passive ticks for a bit. Urgot is really burstable early game after lvl 6 and you need to
take that opportunity since he outscales you tremendously hard. Build for teamfights (Deadman's, Steraks) so you can avoid 1v1ing Urgot
since he has one of the best dueling kits vs Garen.
