WUKONG EASY
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE. Ability Order: Q MAX.
Rush Ironspike Whip into Berserker Greaves.
Wukong's entire kit revolves around extended trades. This is problematic for Wukong since Garen is one of the best champs at nullifying
his extended trades. Wukong also has low sustain so Garen poking him with Q max is really terrible for Wukong. Garen can simply just
auto Q Wukong and walk away. Or even Auto Q E or Q E Ironspike Whip and run away immediately in a straight lane without thinking twice.
Wukong's passive give him armor as he deals damage, his E gives him attack speed which is better in extended fights, and his Q CD gets
reduced by auto attacks. This all isn't really allowed to be done against Garen since Garen just silences him and runs away at the speed
of light with Phase Rush. It gets even worse at Stridebreaker since Wukong can't even follow Garen to auto him. If you play the cooldowns
correctly, there's pretty much no way to lose against Wukong if you disengage every trade quickly. You can perma split vs him and chunk
him out the same way you do in lane. Be careful for his clone when using R, it can bait you out into ulting the wrong Wukong.
