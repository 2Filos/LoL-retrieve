GWEN EASY
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE. Ability Order: Q MAX.
Rush Ironspike Whip into Berserker Greaves. Buy Executioner's Calling and Mortal Reminder after Stridebreaker at some point.
Gwen is a champion that really likes extended trades vs Garen. However, Garen has many ways of shortening trades effectively which
makes the lane pretty easy for Garen after a few points in Q and Ironspike Whip. Gwen has the upperhand in the first few levels since her
base stats are higher than Garen's, but after just 3 points in Q it becomes pretty easy for Garen to just Q E run away and W her Q. All
you really have to do is play around your Phase Rush cooldown and Ironspike Whip so you can Q E Whip over and over to win short trades
and back off to heal with your passive. After Stridebreaker you just Q E Stride active and it's basically impossible for her to do anything
since you will get to chunk her into an HP where she is burstable from Ignite Q E R. The Gwen matchup is all about these short trades in
lane and in side lane while you splitpush. Getting her to a one shottable HP is easy enough if you play around cooldowns correctly. Make
sure to have Phase Rush up post 6 if you are planning to trade with her, she can run you down if your Phase Rush is on cooldown
since it is the only thing keeping you alive from the slows of her R.
