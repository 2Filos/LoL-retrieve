CAMILLE HARD/BAN
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Second Wind, Overgrowth) 
Sorcery (Nimbus Cloak, Scorch) 
SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE.
Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe.

Camille is a matchup that you have to win early game since she outscales you at 1 mythic. However, playing aggressively early game can prove to be very difficult and annoying because of her passive shield, her R, and her E. Since we are taking Grasp, we can start Q lvl 1 and try to win a short trade after her passive is down since this is really the only way to beat her. We can try to bait her passive by trading an auto with her to start her passive shield up and then we can follow her as she kites back to then punish with our Q the moment the shield drops for a trade she won’t like. It is important we W her Q1 when trying to bait her passive in later levels since it can be a lot of burst and wasted HP just for her passive cooldown. It is really important that we are hyper aware of the enemy jungler since she has amazing gank setup with her ult that basically is a death sentence from full hp if you are standing in the middle of the lane with her jungler topside. If Camille ever uses E into you, you can W it to get tenacity and pretty much instantly get out of the stun. This matchup is all about catching her with Q past her passive CD, if we can’t do that then it is doomed. You can also try to Flash on her when she is low HP to surprise her before she can use passive and get a bit of Q and spin damage before she uses passive which you can then respond with Ignite R if she is low enough to one shot her since the true damage goes through her physical damage shield.
