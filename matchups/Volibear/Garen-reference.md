VOLIBEAR MEDIUM
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Conditioning, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH GHOST. Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush
Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip.
Volibear is a very strong laning phase champion and he pretty much can't be beaten unless you go for lots of short trades and you dodge
his E (Thunder Circle thing) by using W on his stun. The main trading pattern usually is just Q E run away while using W on his stun, but
you don't really want to fight Volibear or drop HP because his dive potential with his R disabling the turret and his jungler coming to help
is too great of a threat for the most part. If Volibear's R is active it's safe to just ghost and run away to kite it out until it's on cooldown. Once
you have Ironspike Whip or Stridebreaker, it becomes really easy to short trade with him. However, if he purchases Iceborn Gauntlet, it's
probably better to stick to teamfighting vs that guy. Any champion with Iceborn Gauntlet is a big problem for Garen since it reduces so much
of his damage and the slow is crippling if you don't have Phase Rush available for slow reduction. The lane is mostly just about farming and
not dropping too low of an HP so you can get to Stridebreaker and lvl 11 to teamfight or maybe 1v1 him if you are brave enough.
