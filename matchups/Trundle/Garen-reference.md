TRUNDLE MEDIUM
RUNES: Conqueror, (Triumph Tenacity Last Stand)
Sorcery: Nimbus Cloak, Celerity. 
SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE. 
ABILITY ORDER: E MAX. Rush Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip.

Trundle is one of the most stat checky champs in League of Legends purely because he steals your armor and lowers your AD. He also has incredible sustain if he opts for Vampiric Scepter so it's best to try to burst him from a higher HP with a more aggressive rune page like Conqueror. 

The other rune options only allow you to short trade and run away while Conqueror allows you to go for a higher HP all in which is what you want to do vs Trundle since his sustain is so high. When the wave is on your side you can try to space at the max range of your E and go for an extended trade vs Trundle to set him up for an all by chunking him so he is burstable. 

You can even try to do this at level 1, but you have to be really careful and know your limits decently well since Trundle can run you down really easily with his pillar and Q slow. After 1 or 2 items it's best to not 1v1 Trundle since he gets stronger and stronger in the 1v1 as the game progresses unless you got a decent lead in lane. He is really useless in teamfights so it's best to take him on in those.
