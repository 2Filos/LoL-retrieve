JAYCE HARD
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE. Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush
Ironspike Whip into Berserker’s Greaves.
Jayce is a ranged champion that scales incredibly well and can bully you in lane. For this reason we need to show him a lot of respect in the
early levels and get a lead at some point after we purchase Ironspike Whip. With Q max, you can chase the Jayce down unless he uses
his movement speed gate to kite you which can be problematic. If he uses it when you Q at him, it’s best to disengage the trade and wait
until your Q is up again since his movement speed gate has a longer cooldown than your Q. When you have Ironspike Whip and at least
3 points into Q, you can basically poke Jayce down since he has no sustain and really low base HP which can lead to you getting him low
enough for you to Flash Q E Ignite R him. You run at him with Q and then instantly E Ironspike Whip to run away with Phase Rush since
you don’t want to extend the trade vs him. You can W his burst while doing this and you will effectively never lose the trade if he doesn’t
end up kiting or spacing out your Q. Remember to respect him for the most part and only go for short pokes like this when you have all
of your cooldowns up. If Jayce doesn’t have Phase Rush, he gets outscaled after Stridebreaker since you can hit him with the Stride
slow as he knocks you away in his hammer form. Jayce is really one shottable so the all in threshold is pretty high and he can die from
even 65% HP if you Flash Q E Ignite R.
