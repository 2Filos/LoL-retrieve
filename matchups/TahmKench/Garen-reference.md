TAHM KENCH EASY
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Conditioning/Second Wind, Overgrowth), Inspiration: Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight.
SUMMS: FLASH GHOST ABILITY ORDER: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe.
Tahm Kench is a very weak champion overall with his only main strength being a decent amount of durability with his shield and his base damage on his tongue skill shot. He can also kidnap you into his tower if he stacks on you while you are close to it, but other than that, Tahm Kench struggles being a threat to Garen in laning phase due to his lack of damage. You can pretty much just walk up to this guy and bonk him with a Q whenever you have Grasp ready and he can't really punish it. You just have to make sure he doesn't get his 3 stacks so that he can't stun you or R you. He has pretty decent gank setup with his R as well so that's all you really need to worry about. If you are chasing him down, try to hold your Q for his W which is his channel ability where he teleports through the ground. You can silence him out of it which is really terrible for him. Otherwise just perma split vs him since Trinity Cleaver is broken and if you can't deal enough dmg you can just opt to get a hull cannon under the tower and demolish it if no one is around to punish you.
