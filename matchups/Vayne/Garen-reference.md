VAYNE HARD
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH GHOST. Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush
Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip.
Vayne is a champion that you must survive until Stridebreaker against. You may not be able to 1v1 her, but you will be a lot more useful in
teamfights as a frontline if you didn't die in lane and you got to soak all the XP you needed to get to the special lvl 11 number. If she takes
too much minion aggro or tower damage there's potential for a kill as well but you will mostly be stuck under tower farming as much as you
can. Make sure you don't drop too much HP so you don't get dove by her or her jungler. There's a certain amount of minions you're allowed
to take without getting destroyed. If Vayne ever tumbles forwards you can potentially go for a Q on her and see if she eats your E as well. If
she instantly condemns you it's fine since you won't really get punished all that hard and she will be missing hp that won't come back
since her sustain isn't good at all. It's important to not overextend in lane since Vayne can run you down with multiple autos since her
passive gives her movement speed towards you.
