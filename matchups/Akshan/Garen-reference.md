AKSHAN HARD
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Overgrowth/Unflinching) SUMMS: FLASH GHOST. Ability Order: E MAX. Rush
Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip.
Akshan is a champion with one of the best early games in top lane. He has an insanely good lvl 1-2-3 to the point where you need to give
XP if he decides to level up his hook lvl 2 and use it to zone you off. It can one shot you from nearly full since he runs Ignite as well so you
need to be really careful and start W. Garen outscales Akshan in the 1v1 with Stridebreaker, but getting there without being down lots and
lots of CS is really hard. There's basically no kill opportunities on him unless he takes minion aggro damage or tower shots so you pretty
much have to play for high CS and high HP so you don't get dove. You will be more useful than him in teamfights at level 11 as long as you
aren't turbo behind so you can always be patient and wait for that. The lane is very brutal overall.
