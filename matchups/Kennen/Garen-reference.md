KENNEN HARD
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Unflinching/Overgrowth) SUMMS: FLASH GHOST/GHOST TELEPORT. Ability Order:
E MAX. Rush Berserker Greaves into Ironspike Whip.
Kennen is another matchup where there is no chance of killing him unless he makes a big mistake and takes tower shots or too much
minion aggro. You have to scale up for Stridebreaker. Once you do get it though, if you didn’t die, he gets outscaled quite hard. The problem
with Kennen is that he can go First Strike and bully you hard in lane. He typically ends lane with a 1000 gold lead if he’s good, so it is
quite painful. It all comes down to how well you can cs minions under tower without getting dove by his jungler or even just him since his R
all in is strong. Using W on his shuriken is usually the best idea since that is what does the most damage in his kit. The tenacity also works
on his stuns so it’s good to use if you know he’s going to stun you. It’s a farm lane/wait for jungle to help you overall.
