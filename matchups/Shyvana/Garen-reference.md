SHYVANA EASY
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Conditioning/Second Wind, Overgrowth), Inspiration: Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight.
SUMMS: FLASH GHOST ABILITY ORDER: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe.
Shyvana is one of the weirdest champion dynamics in league. She has a kit that is built around scaling, but is actually pretty strong early
game. Her base stats are incredibly high so it's a good idea to keep the trades really short with just Q and W. Her empowered auto activates
2 PTA autos so it's important to W that. When Shyvana has R, make sure to be really careful since she can dash onto you and use her
empowered projectile ability and it does a ton of damage. Just chill and farm when she has it and go for a Grasp trade here and there without
losing too much hp. After mythic it is pretty much an auto win in side lane 1v1.
