MAOKAI EASY
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Conditioning/Second Wind, Overgrowth), Inspiration: Magical Footwear, Cosmic Insight.
SUMMS: FLASH GHOST ABILITY ORDER: Q MAX. Rush Sheen into Ruby Crystal into Hearthbound Axe.
Maokai is a champion with a mostly weak early game but he does have decent poke when he knocks you away. It's important to Grasp Q him when he knocks you away so you can even out the trade. Maokai is a bit similar to Cho'Gath in the sense that he has great sustain and a decent short trade so it will most likely be a farm lane. You can still try to chunk out Maokai as much as you want since he doesn't really have great kill pressure. He does have good gank setup though so you have to be careful for that. After Trinity Force it should be pretty easy to just perma split vs him and outscale him in side lane. If it is too hard you can shove the wave, take jungle camps, and then out rotate since he will have to respect you.
