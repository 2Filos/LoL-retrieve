RENGAR MEDIUM
RUNES: Grasp of The Undying (Demolish, Bone Plating, Overgrowth) Sorcery: (Nimbus Cloak, Celerity) SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE.
Ability Order: Q MAX. Rush Two Ruby Crytals into Sheen into Tier 1 Boots into Hearthbound Axe.
Rengar is a very strong early game champion that pretty much can't lose the early levels in any way shape or form. This is why it's important
to pay lots of respect to him early on and to hug the wall that is furthest away from the bush. If he ever leaps on you, use your W immediately
on the first part of damage he does. Starting W lvl 1 is recommended too since he can do this right as the lane starts with no counterplay.
It's okay to give CS early since Rengar scales a lot worse compared to you and after one recall he will begin to have issues short trading with
you. Be careful when chasing Rengar into bushes, he can jump to minions from the bush back and forth to kite you and even outplay you
since he will get his stacks to heal up again or use another empowered ability. It's best to avoid walking into bushes with him for that reason
unless you can immediately kill him. After 1 item there is pretty much no way he can win unless you are very behind in laning phase.
