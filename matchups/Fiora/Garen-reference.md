FIORA HARD/BAN
RUNES: Phase Rush, Resolve (Second Wind, Overgrowth) 
SUMMS: FLASH IGNITE. Ability Order: E MAX.
Rush Tiamat. Make sure to buy Executioner's Calling after Stridebreaker for Mortal Reminder. 
(Sit on a Zeal and then finish Phantom Dancer after Mortal Reminder) 

The Fiora matchup is all about good mechanics and spacing with autos and Garen E. 
Fiora’s vital passive is her main tool to win trades vs Garen since he has no way of responding if she hits one of them other than using W and backing off. The way to beat Fiora is to catch her using Q on you and then adjusting your position with a short movement so that she cannot hit the vital. If she hits Q only on you and not the vital, you can then start spinning as she has no way of kiting it out since the movespeed from the vital is her only way of getting out of the spin and she missed it. 

This is the best way to punish Fiora for Garen and you can save your W for her second hit with her empowered auto instead. If you lose a trade, it’s okay to back up and heal with passive since she has no sustain and you do. It’s important that you try to get winning trades like this since Fiora outscales Garen pretty hard unless you build specifically for the 1v1 and play very well. 

After winning a few trades with E, you can try to all in her with Flash Q E R if she’s one shottable (usually around 35% hp) since Flash Q makes it difficult for her to react with her parry. You can also try to force her parry out by starting the all in with Q and if she doesn’t parry the Q at the start, then she is going to die to the silence since you can just press E and R before it runs out. You can only do this if the threat of her dying is great enough to be one shot just from Q E R if she doesn’t parry it. If she does parry your Q, then you can still hit her with E and R since she doesn’t have parry for your R anymore. One great tip against bad Fiora players that don’t react to Garen’s Q animation and instead predict that you are going to hit her with Q is to start your Q and run at Fiora and the moment you get into melee range start clicking around her instead of on her so that you don’t hit Q. Bad Fiora players will use their parry early before you even Q and then when they come out of invulnerability, you can hit them with the Q still to punish it. The matchup is overall about having good micromovements and dodging her vital hits with her Q so you can punish with your spin.
If you want to avoid her hitting R vitals, you can stand right next to a wall in the lane as well and if it’s close enough to the wall, it will be impossible for her to hit the vital. Typically when she Rs though, you want to just run away. Fiora’s teamfight is a lot worse than Garen’s and you also have better wave clear so you can usually clear a wave in side lane far up before she starts split pushing and then run down to an objective fight if you want to not fight her 1v1. Moral of the story, always start trades/all ins with E unless she is low enough to be one shot for not using parry on Q.
